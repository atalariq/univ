## Overview

The TRPL 2021 Revised curriculum spans 8 semesters (4 years) with a total of **~156 SKS** (144 mandatory + 12 elective). Semester 6 is designated as a mandatory MBKM (Merdeka Belajar Kampus Merdeka) semester with an industrial internship. Semester 7 offers both regular and MBKM tracks.

### SKS Legend

- **T** = Teori (Theory) credits
- **P** = Praktik (Practical/Lab) credits
