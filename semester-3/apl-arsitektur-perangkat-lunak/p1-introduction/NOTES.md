## Requirements

1. Functional - **apa yang bisa dilakukan system?**
2. Non-functional - **bagaimana perilaku system?**
   - Maintainability
   - Scalability
   - Reliability
   - Efficiency
   - Security

## Level of Design

1. System Architecture (Very high level) -- Business process
2. Software Architecture (High level) -- Modularity, data sources (files and database), external interface
3. Detailed Design (Low level) -- Data structure and algorithm

## Pattern

Strategi untuk menyelesaikan masalah yang sering muncul; belum sampai ke level implementasi, hanya generalisasi.
